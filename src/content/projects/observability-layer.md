The Observability Layer is a planned workstream for making platform health visible.

The first goal is not enterprise-grade monitoring theatre. The first goal is clarity:

- what is running;
- what is healthy;
- what is exposed;
- what needs attention;
- what changed recently.

The public notes will focus on patterns and decisions:

- lightweight metrics collection;
- dashboard design;
- service status reporting;
- alerting boundaries;
- public-safe reporting;
- observability as part of Infrastructure as Code.

Do not describe this as complete until implementation work actually lands.
