ReplayLab is an experimental space for testing ideas before they become stable platform patterns.

The intent is to keep experiments visible and reusable instead of letting them disappear into local notes, one-off scripts, or half-remembered terminal history.

ReplayLab may cover:

- workflow replay and validation;
- small technical prototypes;
- agent-assisted development experiments;
- UI or tooling ideas;
- operational patterns that need testing before publication.

The project is deliberately exploratory. It should not be presented as a finished product yet.
